# Community code of conduct

Ponte Fotos welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels, in English and Português (Brasil).

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, discuss technical ideas constructively, acknowledge contributions and respect people's privacy. Give actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests and discussions. Maintainer Allan Reis may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock conversations and restrict participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report problematic public content with GitHub's reporting tools. For a concern requiring private handling, ask the maintainer for a private contact without posting sensitive details. Do not publish personal information in a report.

## Português (Brasil)

Respeite as pessoas, discuta ideias com educação e preserve a privacidade. Assédio, discriminação, ameaças, ataques pessoais e divulgação de dados privados não são aceitos. A moderação pode remover conteúdo e restringir participação de forma proporcional. Use as ferramentas de denúncia do GitHub ou solicite contato privado sem expor informações sensíveis.
